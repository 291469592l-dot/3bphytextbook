Overview and Master Formula Sheet

总览与核心公式表

Book 3B is the second half of the compulsory topic Wave Motion. Where Book 3A treated light as rays, Book 3B treats every wave as a vibration that travels, and then uses five phenomena — reflection, refraction, diffraction, interference and stationary waves — to explain water waves, light and sound with the same physics.

必修 3B 是必修课题「波动」的后半部分。3A 把光当作光线处理，3B 则把一切波都看作传播中的振动，再用五个现象 —— 反射、折射、衍射、干涉与驻波 —— 以同一套物理去解释水波、光与声音。

2 Five habits that protect your marks

2 保住分数的五个习惯
  • Name the graph before you read it. s–d says "many particles, one instant"; s–t says "one particle, many instants". A single wrong label loses the whole question.读图前先说清是哪张图。s–d 是「多个质点、同一时刻」；s–t 是「同一质点、多个时刻」。标错一次就整题失分。
  • Write "f unchanged" at every boundary. Reflection, refraction, diffraction and interference never change the frequency; only v and λ change together, keeping v = fλ.在每个界面都写一句「f 不变」。反射、折射、衍射、干涉都不改变频率；只有 v 与 λ 一同改变，使 v = fλ 成立。
  • Check which phenomenon is unique to waves. Reflection and refraction happen to balls and sledges too; only diffraction and interference prove something is a wave.分清哪种现象是波独有的。球与雪橇也会反射与「折射」；只有衍射与干涉能证明某物是波。
  • Compare the fringe patterns. Double slit: evenly spaced, formula Δy = λD/a. Grating: wider spacing for higher orders, formula d sin θn = nλ.比较两种条纹图样。双缝：等距，用 Δy = λD/a。光栅：级次越高间距越大，用 d sin θn = nλ。
  • Halve every echo. Radar, sonar and every other echo measurement give the round trip; divide by two before you answer.回波时间要除以 2。雷达、声呐与所有回声测量给出的都是往返时间，作答前先除以 2。
Exam tip

When a question says explain, the mark scheme wants the physics chain. For "why is this wave a wave?", the chain is: it shows diffraction → it spreads into the shadow of an obstacle → diffraction is shown only by waves → therefore it is a wave.

题目要求解释时，评分标准要的是物理推理链。回答「为什么这是波」时，链条是：它表现出衍射 → 它扩散进障碍物的阴影区 → 只有波才表现出衍射 → 因此它是波。

4.1 Introduction to waves

4.1 波的简介

1 Producing waves

1 产生波
Definition

A wave is a travelling vibration or oscillation that carries energy away from its source.

波是向外传播的振动或振荡，把能量带离波源；能离开波源向外传播的波称为行波（travelling wave）。

Definition

A mechanical wave is a wave that needs a medium to travel through.

机械波必须依靠介质才能传播，如水波、绳波与声波；在真空中不能传播。

2 Energy transmission in waves

2 波传递能量

Push a box into a ball: the ball rolls away, because energy is carried by moving matter. Send a pulse along a spring instead: the coils only vibrate, yet the far end still jerks.

推动箱子撞球，球会滚开，能量靠运动物质带过去。改以弹簧传一个脉冲：弹簧圈只振动不前进，另一端仍被牵动。

Definition

A wave transfers energy in the direction of travel without transferring matter.

波沿传播方向传递能量而不传递物质；质点只在平衡位置附近振动。

Figure 4.1 — In (a) matter is carried away; in (b) only energy travels along the spring.
图 4.1 — (a) 物质被带走；(b) 只有能量沿弹簧传出。
Note

Energy travels in the direction of the wave; the particles stay near their equilibrium positions.

能量沿波的传播方向传递；质点只停在各自的平衡位置附近。

3 Transverse and longitudinal waves

3 横波与纵波
Definition

In a transverse wave the particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of travel.

横波中质点振动方向垂直于传播方向，波形由波峰（crest）与波谷（trough）组成，例如水波与绳波。

Definition

In a longitudinal wave the particles vibrate parallel to the direction of travel.

纵波中质点振动方向平行于传播方向，波形由密部（compression）与疏部（rarefaction）组成，例如声波。

Example 4.4 例题 4.4 — Two ropes joined together

Ropes A and B are uniform and of equal length, but B is heavier than A. They are joined into one long rope; one end is fixed and the free end is shaken steadily at a constant frequency, so a wave travels from A into B. How do the wave speed, frequency and wavelength change?

A、B 两条均匀绳长度相同，B 比 A 重，连接成长绳，一端固定，另一端以恒定频率摆动，波由 A 传入 B。说明波速、频率与波长如何改变。

Solution

第一步（波速） 两绳张力 T 相同，但 B 线密度较大，故波速减小：vB < vA。

第二步（频率） 频率由振源（手）决定，进入新介质后不变。

第三步（波长） 由 v = f λ 且 f 不变，λB = vB / f < λA，波长减小。

a Spreading of waves

衍射只改变波的传播方向；波速 v、频率 f 与波长 λ 都不变。

Sea waves diffract round a breakwater, so a harbour is never perfectly calm. Radio waves also diffract round hills to reach a valley behind them.

Exam tip

Diffraction and interference are properties of waves only. Reflection and refraction also happen to particles, so a phenomenon can be identified as a wave phenomenon if it shows diffraction or interference.

衍射与干涉是波独有的现象；反射与折射其他物体也会有，因此只要某现象表现出衍射或干涉，就可判断它与波有关。

Exam tip

Diffraction changes neither the wavelength, the frequency nor the wave speed. Do not write "the wavelength becomes longer after diffraction" — only the direction of travel changes.

a Superposition of two pulses
a 两个脉冲的叠加
Experiment

Experiment 5e (Adding pulses): lay a long spring on the floor and flick both ends at once, first to the same side, then to opposite sides.

Definition

The principle of superposition states that the resultant displacement equals the sum of the displacements due to each wave alone.

叠加原理：两个脉冲或波重叠时，合位移等于各波单独引起的位移之和。

b Interference of waves
b 波的干涉
Experiment

Experiment 5f (Producing an interference pattern): mount two point vibrators on one vibrating bar to make two sets of circular waves.

实验 5f（产生干涉图样）：把两个点振子装在同一根振动棒上，产生两列圆形波并观察图样。

The two vibrators have the same frequency and a constant phase difference, so they are coherent sources that form a stable pattern.

两个振子频率相同、相位差恒定，故称为相干波源；它们产生的波重叠时形成稳定的干涉图样。

Coherent sources are sources of the same frequency with a constant phase difference.

相干波源是频率相同、相位差恒定的两个波源。

In the pattern some places stay calm all the time, while others vibrate with a larger amplitude than one wave alone.

图样中有些位置始终平静；有些位置振动的振幅比单列波更大。

c Understanding the interference pattern
c 理解干涉图样
i By phase difference
i 用相位差

At X a crest from S1 meets a crest from S2, and at Y a trough meets a trough: the two waves are always in phase and reinforce each other.

X 处来自 S1 的波峰与来自 S2 的波峰相遇，Y 处波谷与波谷相遇：两列波始终同相，互相加强。

At Z a crest from S1 meets a trough from S2, and the particle stays at the equilibrium position.

Z 处来自 S1 的波峰与来自 S2 的波谷相遇，质点停在平衡位置。

Definition

Constructive interference occurs when two waves arrive in phase and reinforce each other.

相长干涉：两列波同相到达，叠加后互相加强，振幅增大。

Definition

Destructive interference occurs when two waves arrive in antiphase and cancel each other.

相消干涉：两列波反相到达，叠加后互相抵消。

Exam tip

At a constructive position the particle still vibrates, only with a larger amplitude; at a destructive position it stays at rest.

相长干涉处的质点不会永远停在波峰或波谷，而是在更大振幅下持续振动；相消干涉处的质点永远静止在平衡位置。

The path difference Δ is the difference between the distances from a point to the two sources.

程差 Δ 是该点到两个波源的距离之差。

n = 0, 1, 2, … · Δ = 0, λ, 2λ, 3λ, …

相消干涉（两波反相到达）
n = 0, 1, 2, … · Δ = 0.5λ, 1.5λ, 2.5λ, …

For two in-phase sources, use the path difference (a difference in distances), not how long one path is.

对两个同相点波源，判据用程差（距离之差），而不是「某条路径有几个波长」。

5.4 Stationary waves

5.4 驻波

Flick the free end of a long stretched string and a wave travels along it. Now fix both ends and flick continuously: a wave still appears, but it stays in the same place. A wave that does not travel is called a stationary wave (or standing wave).

拨动长绳的自由端，波会沿绳传播。若把两端都固定再连续拨动，波依然出现，但它停在原地不动。这种不前进的波称为驻波。

1 Observing stationary waves

1 观察驻波
Experiment

Experiment 5h. Stretch a long spring on the floor with one end fixed to a rigid support. (1) Flick the free end once and watch the pulse reflect at the fixed end. (2) Flick the free end continuously and slowly increase the frequency, watching the pattern on the spring.

实验 5h。把长弹簧放在地上，一端固定在坚固支架上。(1) 拨动自由端一次，观察脉冲在固定端的反射。(2) 连续拨动自由端并逐渐增大频率，观察弹簧上的图样。

As the frequency rises, at certain stages one or more large-amplitude vibrating loops appear on the spring. Snapshots of the spring show that these loops are a wave that does not move forwards or backwards.

随着频率升高，在若干特定阶段弹簧上会出现一个或多个振幅很大的波环。弹簧的快照显示，这些波环是一个不向前也不向后移动的波。

Definition

A stationary wave is a wave pattern that stores energy instead of carrying it from place to place.

驻波是把能量储存起来而不把能量从一处传到另一处的波动图样。

Definition

A node is a point on a stationary wave that does not vibrate at all.

波节是驻波上完全不振动的点。

Definition

An antinode is a point on a stationary wave that vibrates with the largest amplitude.

波腹是驻波上振幅最大的点。

图 5.15 — 固定端处为波节，中间为一个波腹；整个图样不移动。
The distance between two neighbouring nodes (or antinodes) is always 0.5λ. 相邻两波节（或两波腹）之间的距离恒为 0.5λ。
Note

A stationary wave still carries energy — the particles have kinetic and potential energy — but it does not transmit energy from one place to another. The energy is trapped inside the wave region.

驻波仍然携带能量（质点具有动能与势能），但它不把能量从一处传到另一处；能量被局限在波动区域之内。

2 Formation of a stationary wave

2 驻波的形成

At a fixed end a crest is reflected as a trough, and a continuous wave is reflected in the same way; the reflected wave keeps the same v, f and λ. The incident wave and the reflected wave therefore cross each other, superpose, and produce a resultant wave. Only for certain wavelengths does a stationary wave appear — other wavelengths leave the string vibrating irregularly.

在固定端，波峰被反射成波谷，连续波也以同样方式反射；反射波的 v、f、λ 都不变。于是入射波与反射波互相穿过、叠加，产生合成波。只有某些特定波长才形成驻波，其他波长下弦只作不规则振动。

Definition

A stationary wave is formed by two identical waves travelling at the same speed in opposite directions.

驻波由两列速率相同的相同波沿相反方向传播叠加而成。

Figure 5.16 — Two identical waves travelling in opposite directions add up to a stationary wave.
图 5.16 — 两列相同的反向行波叠加成驻波。
At a node the two waves are always in antiphase; at an antinode they are always in phase. 波节处两波始终反相，波腹处两波始终同相。
Quick check 25

Answer: At a fixed end the pulse is reflected inverted — a crest comes back as a trough and vice versa, and it travels in the opposite direction with the same speed, width and amplitude. So the correct diagram is the one whose reflected pulse is upside down.

答案：在固定端，脉冲被倒相反射 —— 波峰变波谷、波谷变波峰，并以相同速率、相同宽度与相同振幅反向传播。故正确图是「反射脉冲上下颠倒」的那一幅。

A stationary wave is formed between two fixed ends. Which of the following can not be a possible waveform? (Consider where the nodes must be.)

答案：固定端必须是波节，且波环数必须为整数，故长度必须满足 L = nλ/2。凡把波腹画在固定端、或波环数非整数的图形都不可能。

3 Frequencies of stationary waves

3 驻波的频率
Experiment

Experiment 5i. Attach one end of an elastic string to a vibrator and the other to a retort stand, keeping the string taut. Drive the vibrator from a signal generator so that the frequency can be controlled. Slowly raise the frequency until a stationary wave of one loop appears and record f; then continue until stationary waves of 2, 3 and 4 loops appear, recording f each time.

实验 5i。把弹性绳一端接振动器、另一端接支架并保持拉紧，用信号产生器驱动振动器以控制频率。缓慢升高频率直到出现一个波环的驻波并记录 f；继续升高频率直到出现 2、3、4 个波环的驻波，每次记录 f。

Stationary waves form only at certain frequencies. If one loop appears at f₁, two loops appear at 2f₁, three at 3f₁, and so on. The lowest of these, f₁, is the fundamental frequency; the others are called the second, third, … harmonics.

驻波只在某些频率下形成。若一个波环时频率为 f₁，则两个波环时为 2f₁、三个波环时为 3f₁……其中最低的 f₁ 称为基频，其余依次称为第二、第三……谐频。

Definition

For a string fixed at both ends, the nth stationary wave has fn = n f₁ and λn = 2L / n.

对两端固定的弦，第 n 个驻波满足 fn = n f₁ 且 λn = 2L / n。

Chapter summary
  • A wavefront joins neighbouring particles vibrating in phase and is perpendicular to the direction of travel; adjacent wavefronts are one wavelength apart.波前连接相邻同相质点，垂直于传播方向；相邻波前相距一个波长。
  • Reflection obeys r = i and changes nothing about v, f or λ; a circular wave reflects as if from an image source the same distance behind the barrier.反射遵守 r = i，v、f、λ 都不变；圆形波反射后看似来自挡板后等距的像波源。
  • At a boundary the frequency is unchanged; a wave entering deeper (faster) water speeds up, lengthens its wavelength and bends away from the normal.跨界面时频率不变；进入更深（更快）的水中会加速、波长变长并偏离法线。
  • Snell's law for waves: sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = λ₁ / λ₂.波的斯涅耳定律：sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = λ₁ / λ₂。

1 Examining the pattern

1 观察图样
Definition

A plane transmission grating is a diffraction grating made of a small glass or plastic plate carrying many equally spaced parallel opaque lines.

平面透射光栅是一种衍射光栅，由一小块玻璃或塑料片制成，上面刻有许多等间距的平行不透明刻线。

相邻两条刻线之间的空隙充当一条缝，所以光栅拥有大量狭缝。每 mm 线数较多的称为细光栅（fine grating），线数较少的称为粗光栅（coarse grating）。

2 Motion of particles

2 质点的运动

位于平衡位置（密部或疏部中心）的质点速率最大；位于最大位移处的质点瞬时静止。相距 nλ 的质点同相；(n + 0.5)λ 的质点反相。

7.2 Nature of Sound

7.2 声音的本性

1 Sound as a wave

1 声音是波

Hold a candle so that its smoke rises in a smooth, steady column. Now put a loudspeaker in front of it and turn the sound on: the smoke rises in a series of zigzags. The air in front of the cone is being pushed back and forth.

把蜡烛放在面前，让烟平稳笔直地上升。然后在蜡烛前放一只喇叭并放声：烟会呈锯齿状上升。喇叭纸盆前方的空气被来回推动。

Definition

Sound is a longitudinal wave: the particles of the medium vibrate along the direction of travel.

声音是纵波：介质中的质点沿传播方向来回振动。

When the loudspeaker is turned on its cone vibrates. Air particles in front of the cone are forced to vibrate to and fro, producing a sound wave that carries energy outwards. We hear the sound when the wave reaches our ears. The waveform of a sound wave is a series of compressions (denser regions) and rarefactions (less dense regions).

喇叭通电后纸盆振动，前方的空气质点被推动来回振动，产生向外携带能量的声波；波到达耳朵时我们就听到声音。声波的波形由一系列密部（较密区域）与疏部（较疏区域）组成。

图 7.4 — 振动的纸盆来回推动空气，发出由密部与疏部组成的纵波。
The air particles themselves do not travel to your ear — only the energy does. 空气质点本身并不会跑到你耳朵里 —— 传播的只是能量。

2 Transmission of sound

2 声音的传播
Definition

Sound is a mechanical wave, so it needs a medium and cannot travel through a vacuum.

声音是机械波，因此需要介质，不能在真空中传播。

Sound can travel through solids, liquids and gases, and the particles of the medium vibrate as the sound passes. Pump the air out of a glass jar containing an electric bell: less and less sound is heard. Sound waves cannot travel through a vacuum, which is why astronauts in space cannot talk to each other directly and must use radio (electromagnetic) waves.

声音可在固体、液体与气体中传播，介质粒子随声波振动。把装有电铃的玻璃钟罩内的空气抽走，听到的铃声越来越小。声波不能在真空中传播，因此太空中的航天员不能直接交谈，必须使用无线电（电磁）波。

The speed of sound depends on the medium: it travels faster in solids than in liquids, and faster in liquids than in gases. In air the speed of sound is independent of frequency, but it does increase with temperature.

声速取决于介质：固体中最快、液体次之、气体最慢。空气中声速与频率无关，但随温度升高而增大。

Definition

Sound shows reflection, refraction, diffraction and interference, and it is a wave because it diffracts and interferes.

b Refraction
b 折射

When sound travels from one medium into another at an angle, refraction occurs because the wave speed changes. A balloon filled with carbon dioxide (in which sound is slower than in air) acts like a lens and focuses the sound to a point, where a loud sound is detected.

声从一种介质斜射入另一种介质时，因波速改变而发生折射。充满二氧化碳（声速比空气慢）的气球像一块透镜，把声聚焦到一点，在該点可测到响亮的声。

Note

Cathode-ray oscilloscope (CRO). Two loudspeakers S₁ and S₂ driven by the same signal generator produce alternate loud and soft sounds along PQ. (a) How can the separation between two consecutive loud sounds along PQ be increased? (b) R is 1.2 m from S₁ and 1.56 m from S₂, and the wavelength is 0.24 m. Is a loud or a soft sound heard at R?

两只由同一信号产生器驱动的喇叭 S₁、S₂，沿 PQ 交替出现响亮与轻微的声音。(a) 如何增大沿 PQ 相邻两个响亮处之间的距离？(b) R 距 S₁ 1.2 m、距 S₂ 1.56 m，波长为 0.24 m。R 处听到响亮还是轻微的声音？

Solution

(a) Increase the wavelength (lower the frequency), or decrease the separation between S₁ and S₂. Increasing the speaker separation would narrow the pattern instead.

(a) 增大波长（降低频率），或减小 S₁ 与 S₂ 的间距。增大喇叭间距反而会使图样变窄。

(b) Δ = 1.56 − 1.2 = 0.36 m. Divide by λ: 0.36 / 0.24 = 1.5, so Δ = 1.5λ = (n + 0.5)λ. The waves arrive in antiphase, so destructive interference occurs and a soft sound is heard.

(b) Δ = 1.56 − 1.2 = 0.36 m。除以 λ：0.36 / 0.24 = 1.5，即 Δ = 1.5λ = (n + 0.5)λ。两波反相到达，发生相消干涉，听到轻微的声音。

Example 7.10 例题 7.10 — DSE practice: interference pattern of sound

P and Q are two loudspeakers producing sound waves in phase. A loud sound is detected at M, which is 5.4 m from P and 5.6 m from Q; O is equidistant from P and Q. Which of the following statements must be correct? (1) The wavelength of the sound waves is 20 cm. (2) The fringe-separation equation Δy = λD / a cannot be used here because that equation applies only to light. (3) If the sound waves from P and Q were in antiphase, a soft sound would be detected at M.

P、Q 两只喇叭发出同相声波。在距 P 5.4 m、距 Q 5.6 m 的 M 处听到响亮的声音；O 与 P、Q 等距。下列哪项必然正确？(1) 声波波长为 20 cm。(2) 此处不能用条纹间距公式 Δy = λD / a，因为该公式只适用于光。(3) 若 P、Q 反相，则 M 处将听到轻微的声音。

Solution

(1) Not necessarily correct. A loud sound at M only tells us that Δ = nλ with Δ = 0.2 m; we do not know how many maxima lie between O and M, so n is unknown and λ cannot be found.

(1) 不一定正确。M 处响亮只说明 Δ = nλ（此处 Δ = 0.2 m）；我们不知道 O 与 M 之间有几个极大，n 未知，故求不出 λ。

(2) Incorrect. The equation is not restricted to light — it applies to any wave. However its conditions (Δy << D and a << D) are not satisfied here, so it cannot be used in this particular set-up.

(2) 错。该公式并不限于光 —— 它对任何波都适用。但它的条件（Δy << D 与 a << D）在此并不满足，因此本情形不能使用。

(3) Correct. If the sources are in antiphase, the waves arrive at M in antiphase as well (the path difference is unchanged), so destructive interference occurs and a soft sound is heard. Every maximum becomes a minimum and vice versa.

(3) 对。若两波源反相，则两波到达 M 时也反相（程差不变），于是发生相消干涉，听到轻微的声音。所有极大变极小、极小变极大。

Answer: B — (3) only.

答案：B —— 只有 (3)。

Exam tip

Whenever a question about sound interference gives you two distances, the first step is always to form the path difference and divide it by λ. If the answer is a whole number the sound is loud; if it ends in .5 the sound is soft.

凡是给出两个距离的声干涉题，第一步永远是求程差并除以 λ。若结果为整数则声音响亮；若为「.5」结尾则声音轻微。

Exam tip

Sound is a wave because it diffracts and interferes — not because it reflects or refracts. Balls bounce and sledges change direction too, so reflection and refraction prove nothing about wave nature.

声音是波，是因为它能衍射与干涉，而不是因为会反射或折射。球会反弹、雪橇会改变方向，故反射与折射并不能证明波动性。

  • Wave speed increases with tension and decreases with mass per unit length; when a wave crosses into a denser string, f stays the same but v and λ fall.波速随张力增大而增大、随线密度增大而减小；进入更重（线密度更大）的绳时 f 不变，v 与 λ 都减小。
  • Every particle has the same amplitude, period and frequency as the wave. Particles nλ apart are in phase; (n − 0.5)λ apart are in antiphase.每个质点的振幅、周期、频率都与波相同。相距 nλ 的质点同相；相距 (n − 0.5)λ 的质点反相。
  • A particle has maximum speed at the equilibrium position and is momentarily at rest at a crest or a trough.质点在平衡位置速率最大，在波峰或波谷瞬时静止。
  • s–d graph: many particles, one instant → read A and λ. s–t graph: one particle, many instants → read A and T. Neither gives the direction of travel or the other pair.s–d 图：多个质点、同一时刻 → 读 A 与 λ。s–t 图：同一质点、多个时刻 → 读 A 与 T。两者都不能给出传播方向，也不能同时给出另一对量。

2 Chapter 5 — Wave phenomena and stationary waves

2 第 5 章 —— 波动现象与驻波
Must know
  • A wavefront joins neighbouring particles vibrating in phase and is always perpendicular to the direction of travel; adjacent wavefronts are λ apart.波前连接相邻的同相振动质点，并总是垂直于传播方向；相邻波前相距 λ。
  • Reflection: r = i (angles between the travelling direction and the normal); f, v and λ are unchanged. A circular wave is reflected as if it came from a source at the same distance behind the barrier.反射：r = i（角度为传播方向与法线的夹角）；f、v、λ 都不变。圆形波反射后看似来自挡板后等距的一点。
  • Refraction: f unchanged; deeper water → faster → longer λ → bends away from the normal. Shallower water → slower → shorter λ → bends towards the normal.折射：f 不变；深水 → 更快 → λ 更长 → 偏离法线；浅水 → 更慢 → λ 更短 → 偏向法线。
  • Snell's law for waves: sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = λ₁ / λ₂.波的斯涅耳定律：sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = λ₁ / λ₂。
  • Diffraction: spreading into the shadow of an obstacle; v, f, λ unchanged; more spreading for a longer λ and a narrower gap.衍射：波扩散进障碍物的阴影区；v、f、λ 都不变；波长越长、缝越窄，扩散越明显。
  • Superposition: displacements add. Two sources are coherent when they keep a constant phase difference — only then is a steady interference pattern obtained.叠加：位移相加。两波源相位差恒定时才是相干波源 —— 只有此时才能得到稳定的干涉图样。
  • Path-difference test (two sources in phase): Δ = nλ → constructive; Δ = (n + 0.5)λ → destructive. Widen the pattern by increasing λ or decreasing the source separation.程差判据（两波源同相）：Δ = nλ → 相长；Δ = (n + 0.5)λ → 相消。要得到更宽的图样，可增大波长或减小波源间距。
  • Stationary wave: two identical waves travelling in opposite directions. Node spacing = λ/2; a loop (antinode to node span) = λ/2; L = nλ/2 and fn = n f₁. Particles in the same loop are in phase, adjacent loops in antiphase, and energy is not transmitted.驻波：两列相同的反向行波叠加。相邻波节间距 = λ/2；一个波环 = λ/2；L = nλ/2、fn = n f₁。同一波环内质点同相，相邻波环反相，且不传递能量。

3 Chapters 6 and 7 — Light and sound

3 第 6、7 章 —— 光与声音
Must know
  • Light is a transverse electromagnetic wave, wavelength 400–700 nm. It is a wave because it diffracts and interferes.光是横电磁波，波长 400–700 nm。它之所以是波，是因为它能衍射与干涉。
  • Double slit: a bright central fringe, a symmetric pattern, and evenly spaced fringes with Δy = λD / a (valid when D >> a and θn is small).双缝：有中央明纹、图样对称、条纹等距，Δy = λD / a（需 D >> a 且 θn 很小）。
  • Grating: much brighter and much wider than a double slit; fringes are not evenly spaced; d sin θn = nλ, yn = D tan θn, highest order n ≤ d/λ.光栅：比双缝更亮、图样更宽；条纹不等距；d sin θn = nλ、yn = D tan θn、最高级次 n ≤ d/λ。
  • All EM waves are transverse, need no medium, travel at c in a vacuum, carry energy, obey v = fλ and show all four wave phenomena.所有电磁波都是横波、不需介质、真空中以 c 传播、携带能量、遵守 v = fλ，并表现出全部四种波动现象。
  • Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave; it cannot travel through a vacuum; vsolid > vliquid > vgas; in air v is independent of frequency.声音是纵机械波，不能在真空传播；v固体 > v液体 > v气体；空气中声速与频率无关。
  • Audible range 20 Hz – 20 kHz; ultrasound > 20 kHz, travelling at the same speed as audible sound in air. Sonar and radar: d = ct / 2.可听范围 20 Hz – 20 kHz；超声波 > 20 kHz，在空气中与可听声同速。声呐与雷达：d = ct / 2。
  • Pitch ↔ frequency, loudness ↔ amplitude, quality ↔ waveform. Sound intensity level is measured in dB; 0 dB is the threshold of hearing, not silence; above 140 dB causes permanent damage.音调 ↔ 频率、响度 ↔ 振幅、音品 ↔ 波形。声强级以 dB 为单位；0 dB 是听觉阈而非「没有声音」；超过 140 dB 会造成永久损伤。

Named definitions and key ideas定义与要点

  1. A wave is a travelling vibration or oscillation that carries energy away from its source.
  2. A mechanical wave is a wave that needs a medium to travel through.
  3. A wave transfers energy in the direction of travel without transferring matter.
  4. In a transverse wave the particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of travel.
  5. In a longitudinal wave the particles vibrate parallel to the direction of travel.
  6. The amplitude is the maximum displacement of a particle from its equilibrium position.
  7. The wavelength is the shortest distance over which the waveform repeats itself.
  8. The period is the time taken to produce one complete wave.
  9. The frequency is the number of complete waves produced per second.
  10. The wave speed is the distance travelled by the wave per second.
  11. The speed of a wave along a stretched spring depends on the tension and the mass per unit length.
  12. In a travelling transverse wave, all particles vibrate with the same amplitude, period and frequency as the wave.
  13. Particles a whole number of wavelengths apart are in phase.
  14. Particles separated by an odd number of half wavelengths, 0.5λ, 1.5λ, …, are in antiphase.
  15. Particles whose separation is neither a whole nor a half number of wavelengths are out of phase.
  16. To find how a particle moves, compare the waveform a little earlier or later, or let it repeat a neighbour's earlier displacement.
  17. A particle moves fastest at the equilibrium position and is momentarily at rest at a crest or a trough.
  18. An s–d graph plots the displacement of all particles against their distance from a reference particle.
  19. Only the amplitude A and the wavelength λ can be read from an s–d graph.
  20. The waveform is the shape of the wave, that is, the displacements of all particles at one instant.
  21. An s–t graph plots the displacement of one particle against time.
  22. Two particles are in phase when they always move in the same direction and reach the crest together.
  23. Two particles are antiphase when their displacements are always opposite, so one graph is the other flipped.
  24. A ripple tank is a shallow transparent tray of water with a vibrator that produces water waves for study.
  25. A plane wave is a wave whose wavefronts are parallel straight lines.
  26. A circular wave is a wave whose wavefronts are circles centred on a point source.
  27. A wavefront is a line joining adjacent particles that vibrate in phase.
  28. Reflection is the bouncing back of a wave when it meets a boundary between two media.
  29. The law of reflection states that the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence, r = i.
  30. After reflection the frequency, the wave speed and the wavelength of the wave are all unchanged.
  31. A circular wave reflected by a straight barrier appears to come from a point image I behind the barrier.
  32. Refraction is the change in the direction of travel of a wave caused by a change in its speed.
  33. Water waves travel more slowly in shallow water, so the wave speed v decreases on entering a shallow region.
  34. The frequency of a wave is fixed by its source, so it does not change on refraction.
  35. Entering a slower medium a wave bends towards the normal, so the angle of refraction r is smaller than the angle of incidence i.
  36. Entering a faster medium a wave bends away from the normal, so r is greater than i.
  37. A wave travelling along the normal is not refracted; its direction of travel does not change.
  38. Snell's law states that for refraction at a boundary, sin i / sin r is a constant.
  39. Diffraction is the bending of waves round the edges of an obstacle and their spreading into the shadow region behind it.
  40. The degree of diffraction depends on the ratio of the wavelength to the slit width (or the size of the obstacle): the larger the ratio, the more obvious the diffraction.
  41. The principle of superposition states that the resultant displacement equals the sum of the displacements due to each wave alone.
  42. Coherent sources are sources of the same frequency with a constant phase difference.
  43. Constructive interference occurs when two waves arrive in phase and reinforce each other.
  44. Destructive interference occurs when two waves arrive in antiphase and cancel each other.
  45. The path difference Δ is the difference between the distances from a point to the two sources.
  46. A nodal line is a line along which every point undergoes destructive interference.
  47. An antinodal line is a line along which every point undergoes constructive interference.
  48. The larger the wavelength, or the smaller the source separation, the wider the spacing between the nodal lines.
  49. A stationary wave is a wave pattern that stores energy instead of carrying it from place to place.
  50. A node is a point on a stationary wave that does not vibrate at all.
  51. An antinode is a point on a stationary wave that vibrates with the largest amplitude.
  52. A stationary wave is formed by two identical waves travelling at the same speed in opposite directions.
  53. For a string fixed at both ends, the nth stationary wave has fn = n f₁ and λn = 2L / n.
  54. Particles in the same loop of a stationary wave are in phase; particles in adjacent loops are in antiphase.
  55. In a stationary wave all particles reach their maximum displacements at the same instant.
  56. Diffraction is the spreading of waves when they pass through a narrow gap or around an obstacle.
  57. Interference is the superposition of two coherent waves, giving a bright fringe where they arrive in phase.
  58. Light is a transverse electromagnetic wave; visible light has wavelengths from about 400 nm to 700 nm.
  59. A fringe is one bright or dark band of the interference pattern formed by the two slits.
  60. Fringe separation Δy is the distance between the centres of two neighbouring bright fringes.
  61. Two sources are coherent if they emit waves of the same frequency with a constant phase difference.
  62. The path difference is the extra distance one wave travels compared with the other to reach a point.
  63. At a bright fringe the path difference equals a whole number of wavelengths, so the waves arrive in phase.
  64. A plane transmission grating is a diffraction grating made of a small glass or plastic plate carrying many equally spaced parallel opaque lines.
  65. In a grating pattern, light diffracts at every slit and the diffracted waves from all slits interfere constructively in certain directions, giving bright fringes.
  66. The fringe separation of a grating pattern increases when the grating spacing is smaller, the wavelength is longer, or the grating-to-screen distance is larger.
  67. The grating spacing d is the separation between two adjacent slits of a grating.
  68. The angle of diffraction of the nth-order bright fringe is the angle between that fringe and the central bright fringe.
  69. With white light, the central bright fringe is white and each higher order is a coloured spectrum, violet on the inner side and red on the outer side.
  70. A grating transmits much more light than a double slit, so its bright fringes are brighter, and because d is far smaller than a they are much wider.
  71. Electromagnetic waves are transverse waves made up of changing electric and magnetic fields that travel through a vacuum at 3.00 × 108 m s−1.
  72. All electromagnetic waves are transverse, need no medium, carry energy, obey v = f λ, and show reflection, refraction, diffraction and interference.
  73. A longitudinal wave consists of a series of compressions and rarefactions, and its particles vibrate along the direction of travel.
  74. A compression is a region where the particles of the medium crowd together, and a rarefaction is a region where they spread out.
  75. A displacement–time (s–t) graph plots the displacement of one particle against time, so its amplitude and period can be read directly.
  76. A displacement–distance (s–d) graph shows the displacements of all the particles at one instant, so its amplitude and wavelength can be read.
  77. Sound is a longitudinal wave: the particles of the medium vibrate along the direction of travel.
  78. Sound is a mechanical wave, so it needs a medium and cannot travel through a vacuum.
  79. Sound shows reflection, refraction, diffraction and interference, and it is a wave because it diffracts and interferes.
  80. The audible range is the range of sound frequencies a normal human ear can hear, about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz.
  81. Ultrasound is sound of frequency higher than 20 kHz, above the upper limit of the audible range.
  82. A sonar sends out ultrasound pulses and detects the echoes to locate objects under water.
  83. A musical note is a sound produced by a regular vibration, so its waveform repeats regularly.
  84. Noise is a sound produced by an irregular vibration, so its waveform does not repeat regularly.
  85. The pitch of a note tells how high or low it sounds; the higher the frequency, the higher the pitch.
  86. The loudness of a sound tells how loud it is; the greater the amplitude, the louder the sound.
  87. The quality of a note is the feature that distinguishes the same note played on different instruments.
  88. The sound intensity level is a scale for describing how loud a sound is, measured in decibels (dB).
